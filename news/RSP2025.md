# Semester programme 2025

In the fall of 2025 CWI hosted a research semester programme on the synergies between
numerical linear algebra and scientific machine learning, organized by Victorita Dolean,
Jemima Tabeart, Alexander Heinlein, Tristan van Leeuwen and Benjamin Sanderse.

<https://www.cwi.nl/en/events/research-semester-programmes/bridging-numerical-analysis-and-scientific-machine-learning/>
