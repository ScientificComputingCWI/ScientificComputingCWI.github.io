# Royal decoration for Barry Koren

Barry Koren, advisor to the group, was appointed **Officer of the Order of
Orange-Nassau** following his farewell speech at Eindhoven University of Technology on
3 April 2025, marking his retirement after thirteen years there.

The decoration recognises his contributions to research in applied mathematics, to
teaching, and to university governance.
