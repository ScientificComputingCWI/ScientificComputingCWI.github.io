# ML4FLUIDS 2026 at CWI

CWI hosted the **3rd ERCOFTAC workshop on Machine Learning for Fluid Dynamics**
in Amsterdam on **4–6 March 2026**.

The workshop brought together work on machine learning applied to fluid dynamics:
data-driven and reduced-order modelling, flow control, super-resolution, uncertainty
quantification, and ML-accelerated solvers. Selected presentations were invited to
submit to a special issue of *Flow, Turbulence and Combustion*.

The local organizing committee was Benjamin Sanderse (CWI), Wouter Edeling (CWI),
Richard Dwight (TU Delft), Anh Khoa Doan (Imperial College), Bernat Font (TU Delft),
Tyler Buchanan (TU Delft), Magdalena Yates (ERCOFTAC) and Nada Mitrovic (CWI).

For more information, see <https://ml4fluids2026.github.io>
