# NUM-SCARS 2026 at CWI

CWI hosted the inaugural **NUM-SCARS** workshop in Amsterdam on **22–23 January 2026**,
launching ERCOFTAC Special Interest Group 55 on *advanced NUmerical Methods for SCAle
Resolving Simulations*.

The workshop set out to bring together researchers and practitioners working on
numerical methods for scale-resolving simulations, with keynotes on discretization
methods, closure models and benchmarking, alongside discussions of joint projects for
the new interest group.

It was organized by Benjamin Sanderse (CWI) together with Francesco Capuano and
F. Xavier Trias (Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya), Alessandro Colombo (Università
degli Studi di Bergamo) and Artur Palha (TU Delft).

For more information, see <https://num-scars.github.io>
