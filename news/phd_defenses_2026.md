# Two PhD defences in the group

Two members of the group defended their theses in May 2026, both at Eindhoven
University of Technology.

**Toby van Gastelen** defended *Structure-Preserving Data-Driven Methods for Modeling
Turbulent Flows* on 19 May 2026
([defence](https://casa.win.tue.nl/home/event/defense-toby-van-gastelen/),
[thesis](https://research.tue.nl/nl/publications/structure-preserving-data-driven-methods-for-modeling-turbulent-f/)).

**Syver Døving Agdestein** defended *Data-driven discrete closure models for large-eddy
simulation of incompressible turbulence* on 28 May 2026
([defence](https://casa.win.tue.nl/home/event/defense-syver-doving-agdestein/),
[thesis](https://research.tue.nl/nl/publications/data-driven-discrete-closure-models-for-large-eddy-simulation-of-/)).

Both theses concern closure models for turbulence, covering energy-conserving neural
networks, a-posteriori learning, discretization consistency and symmetry-preserving
models.
